# Tanukifin Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

Tanukifin is a free, open-source app for watching media from a **Jellyfin server that you or someone you trust runs**. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What Tanukifin collects
Tanukifin has no developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking or automatic crash uploads. Your selected server and resources it references can receive network requests as described below.

## What is stored on your device
To work, the app saves the following only on your TV or device:
- The address of the Jellyfin server you connect to
- Your Jellyfin login session (an access token, not your password)
- Your app settings, such as player and subtitle preferences

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this.

## What is sent to your Jellyfin server
The app connects to the Jellyfin server you enter. Like every Jellyfin app, it sends that server:
- Your username and password when you sign in
- What you watch and your playback progress, so the server can resume playback and mark items as watched
- Basic device information (device name, app name and version) so the server can list the device in its dashboard

That server is not operated by the Tanukifin developer. How it handles data is decided by whoever runs it.

## Children
Tanukifin is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.

## Contact
Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/TRobboJ/Tanukifin/issues

Crash details are saved locally after a crash and displayed on the next launch. Review and redact server addresses or other sensitive details before sharing a screenshot in an issue. Remote resources referenced by your server themes/plugins can contact their respective providers.
